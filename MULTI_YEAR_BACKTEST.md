## Key Findings

### 1. The Model Works — But Not How We Expected
The scoring model is profitable (+$845 across 5 years, 77.8% win rate) but the edge comes almost entirely from:
- **Coaching ATS (Hurley):** 9-1, +$440
- **KenPom underseeding:** 7-1, +$350
- **FT% in close games:** 5-1, +$245
- **Elite Eight underdog:** 5-3, +$95

### 2. The Model's Losers Are Concentrated
All 8 losses come from just two factors:
- **12-seed trend + home court:** 0-3 (-$275)
- **E8 underdog (when it misses):** 0-3 (-$220)
- **Gonzaga E8 in 2023 (mechanical E8 dog rule):** 0-1 (-$110)
- **Kansas championship 2022 (half-point loss):** 0-1 (-$55)

### 3. The Travel Factor Doesn't Work for Betting
Despite strong academic evidence that travel affects outcomes, the betting market appears to already price this in. Travel-boosted bets went 0-3.

### 4. The 12-Seed Trend Is a Trap
The famous "12 beats 5" narrative may be real for SU upsets, but the ATS data doesn't support it as a betting edge. In our backtest, 12-seed-driven bets went 0-2. Across the broader data, 12-seeds went 0-4 ATS in 2023, 1-3 in 2022, 2-2 in 2021.

### 5. The Model Is Ultra-Conservative
36 bets across 5 tournaments (315+ total games) = **betting only 11.4% of games**. This is actually a strength — the model waits for strong convergence and rarely forces a play.

### 6. Realistic Forward Expectations
- **With all factors:** 75-80% win rate, ~30% ROI per tournament
- **Without 12-seed/travel:** 85-90% win rate, ~60% ROI per tournament
- **Caution:** Small sample (36 bets). Need 100+ bets for statistical confidence. The Hurley factor alone accounts for 28% of all bets and 52% of all profits — if Hurley retires or UConn declines, the model loses its primary edge.

## Recommended Model Adjustments for 2026

Based on this 5-year backtest:

1. **REMOVE** the 12-seed factor as a standalone trigger. Only play 12-seeds if 3+ other factors converge (score +5 without the +3).
2. **REMOVE** the travel/proximity factor as a score booster. Use it only as a tiebreaker, not as primary signal.
3. **KEEP** the Elite Eight underdog rule but reduce from +3 to +2 (it went 5-3, profitable but volatile).
4. **KEEP** Hurley/coaching ATS as the strongest factor (+3).
5. **KEEP** KenPom underseeding as a strong factor (+2).
6. **KEEP** FT% in close games (≤4 spread) as the best close-game predictor (+3).
7. **ADD** a "KenPom top-3 as 1-seed" factor (+2) — UConn 2024, Baylor 2021, and Gonzaga 2021/2022 all performed well.
