## Flags & Corrections Applied

### Flag 1: "80%+ public = 2-11 ATS" (Claim 6)
- **Issue:** Neither Sports Insights nor Action Network uses an 80% threshold
- **Resolution:** Strategy and scoring model cite the verifiable **75%+ threshold (31-44-2 ATS)** from Covers.com and the **<40% contrarian side (19-9 ATS)** from Sports Insights. The 80% figure is noted as directionally supported but not independently verifiable.

### Flag 2: "Reverse line movement 272-203 (57.3%)" (Claim 10)
- **Issue:** Cited Sports Insights URL covers steam moves, not RLM. The 272-203 figure is from a narrow 2007-08 tracking blog.
- **Resolution:** Scoring model describes RLM as a "well-established sharp money indicator" without citing the specific dated record. The concept is sourced from Sports Insights' RLM methodology page and Action Network's primer.

### Flag 3: "Small Elite Eight favorites 1-10-1 ATS" (Claim 7)
- **Issue:** VSiN reports 3-10-1 for favorites ≤4 pts, not 1-10-1
- **Resolution:** Scoring model updated to cite VSiN's 3-10-1 figure and the broader 17-33-1 since 1998.

### Flag 4: FT% ATS source attribution (Claim 2)
- **Issue:** NCAA.com article cited does not contain ATS data
- **Resolution:** Primary source corrected to Yardbarker/Action Network primer, which contains the exact stat.
